Key Delta Landmarks

Strategic Watchtowers & Stops

Protected platforms engineered for panoramic wildlife viewing over freshwater watering holes.

01

Dobanki Canopy Walk

A half-kilometer elevated wire-mesh walk suspended 20 feet above ground. Walk freely while deer and tigers roam below.

02

Sudhanyakhali Watchtower

The highest recorded statistical probability for spotting the Royal Bengal Tiger drinking at the sweetwater pond.

03

Netidhopani Historic Ruins

Centuries-old mythological Shiva temple ruins associated with the legend of Behula and Lakhindar deep in the core zone.

04

Bonnie Camp Estuary

The tallest watchtower in Sundarbans (50 ft). Overlooks vast tidal mudflats and the meeting point of open rivers.
